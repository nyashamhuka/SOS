Machke Boarding
What we offer

Structured learning and residential life that builds character.

Machke Boarding provides comprehensive academic programs, secure residential facilities, mentorship, and extracurricular opportunities that help students grow into confident, independent leaders.

Academic Rigor

Strong curriculum across sciences, humanities, and languages with dedicated study time and tutorial support.

Residential Care

Secure dormitories with resident staff, wellness programs, and a structured daily routine that supports student well-being.

Leadership Development

Mentorship programs, student councils, and leadership roles teach responsibility and community engagement.

Boarding Facilities

Modern residential and academic spaces designed for student comfort and success.

Dormitories

Shared rooms with heating, bedding, study desks, and common areas for socializing and recreation.

Study Halls

Quiet study environments with supervised homework time, internet access, and library resources.

Dining Hall

Daily meals prepared on-site with variety, nutrition, and accommodation for dietary preferences.

Sports & Activities

Volleyball, football, debate, music, and clubs available during free time and weekends.
