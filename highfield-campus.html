Highfield Campus
What we offer

Advanced curriculum focused on science, technology, and innovation.

Highfield Campus offers specialized pathways in STEM, digital literacy, engineering, and creative arts, combined with strong liberal arts education for well-rounded students.

STEM Pathways

Advanced courses in biology, chemistry, physics, computer science, and mathematics with lab work and research projects.

Robotics & Engineering

Hands-on programs in robotics, coding, design thinking, and engineering challenges with state-of-the-art equipment.

Creative Arts

Digital design, visual arts, media production, and performance programs alongside academic excellence.

Campus Resources

Modern facilities and equipment designed for hands-on, project-based learning.

Science Labs

Equipped chemistry, biology, and physics labs for experiments, research, and discovery-based learning.

Tech Hub

Computer lab, coding stations, and digital design software for programming, web design, and media projects.

Maker Space

3D printers, robotics kits, electronics, and tools for building, prototyping, and engineering projects.

Library & Media

Digital resources, journals, textbooks, and multimedia materials for research and independent learning.
